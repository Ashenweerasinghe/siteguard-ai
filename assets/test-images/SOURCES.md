## Known gaps

**Stock libraries do not photograph safety violations.** Almost every
construction image on Pexels and Unsplash shows a compliant, posed site, because
that is what clients buy. The edge and walkway sets above are genuine; the PPE
set is the weakest, and `ppe_concern_02_clear.jpg` is a duplicate because no
second usable photo existed.

The fix is ten minutes with a phone on a real site: shoot one photo per hazard
type, plus a matching "after" shot of the same spot once it has been put right.
A real before/after pair of the *same location* is the one thing stock cannot
provide, and it is what makes the closure-verification demo convincing.

Faces are visible in several of these photos. The Pexels License covers the
photographer's copyright, not the depicted person's likeness rights — fine for a
hackathon demo, worth reviewing before any commercial use.
